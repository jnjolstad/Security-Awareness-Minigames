# Puzzle 1 — "The Tailgate"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Mode** | Physical |
| **Position in chain** | Entry point → unlocks Puzzle 2 |
| **Output** | Desk cabinet **key** + Alex's Note #1 |

---

### 1. Puzzle Name
**The Tailgate** — *"Who walked in without permission?"*

### 2. Story Purpose
Players must gain **physical access** to Alex Mercer's locked workstation. The reception desk holds a
lockbox containing the key to her desk. To open it, players review the day's **visitor log** and find
the one person who got in by breaking every visitor rule — the same kind of physical-access failure
that let the attacker into NIMBUS. It sets the tone: *the breach started at the front door.*

### 3. Security Topic
Physical Security · **Tailgating** · Visitor Management.

### 4. Learning Objective
Players learn that physical security is the first layer of cyber defense: visitors must **sign in and
out, have a named host, and be escorted**. Someone who skips these is a tailgating/piggybacking risk and
should be challenged and reported.

### 5. Setup Instructions
1. Place the **lockbox** on the reception desk; set its combination to **2071**.
2. Inside the lockbox, place the **desk-cabinet key** and **Alex's Note #1** (text below).
3. Mount the **Visitor Policy poster** above the desk.
4. Put the **Visitor Sign-In Log** (page below) in a binder or clipboard on the desk.
5. Add set dressing: a pen on a chain, blank badges, a lanyard.

### 6. Materials Needed
- 1 × 4-digit combination lockbox (set to 2071)
- 1 × desk-cabinet key (placed inside)
- 1 × printed **Visitor Sign-In Log** (artifact A1)
- 1 × printed **Visitor Policy poster** (artifact A2)
- 1 × printed **Alex's Note #1** (artifact A3)
- Set dressing (badges, lanyard, pen)

### 7. Exact Puzzle Layout

**Artifact A2 — Visitor Policy poster (the rule set):**
> **NIMBUS VISITOR POLICY**
> Every visitor MUST: ① **Sign in AND out.** ② **Have a named NIMBUS host.** ③ **Be escorted at all times.**
> 🚩 A visitor who breaks **all three** rules is an immediate security risk.
> *Report their **badge number** to security.*

**Artifact A1 — Visitor Sign-In Log (Friday):**

| Time In | Visitor | Company | Host | Badge # | Time Out | Escorted? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 08:15 | A. Lund | CourierCo | Reception | V-1043 | 08:40 | Yes |
| 09:02 | **J. Price** | "Delivery" | — | **V-2071** | — | **No** |
| 09:30 | M. Webb | NimbusIT (contractor) | I. Shaw | EXT-4471 | — | Yes |
| 10:30 | S. Haig | AcmeAudit | D. Cole | V-1119 | 11:45 | Yes |
| 13:05 | R. Okafor | PrintPro | Reception | V-1204 | 13:50 | Yes |

Only **J. Price** breaks **all three** rules (no host, no sign-out, not escorted). Badge **V-2071** →
lockbox code **2071**. *(Note: M. Webb misses sign-out only — quiet foreshadowing for Puzzle 7, not the
answer here.)*

**Artifact A3 — Alex's Note #1 (inside lockbox):**
> *"You got past the door — that's exactly how **he** got in, too. My screen is next. Ignore the
> yellow note; it lies. — A."*

### 8. How Players Discover It
The reception desk is the first thing inside the door. The lockbox is obviously a lock to open; the
poster and visitor log are the only readable clues nearby, pointing players to find a "badge number."

### 9. How Players Solve It
Players read the policy poster (three rules), scan the visitor log, and identify the single visitor who
violated **all three** rules. That visitor's badge number is the 4-digit lockbox code.

### 10. Solution
**J. Price** broke every rule → badge **V-2071** → enter **2071** → lockbox opens → retrieve desk key +
Alex's Note #1.

### 11. Hint 1 (nudge to the place)
*"Alex always said security starts at the front door. That policy on the wall isn't just decoration —
compare it against who actually signed in today."*

### 12. Hint 2 (reveal the method)
*"Three rules. Find the one visitor who broke **all three** — no host, never signed out, no escort. Their
badge number is what you need."*

### 13. Hint 3 (all but the answer)
*"Look at the 09:02 delivery — 'J. Price.' No host, no sign-out, not escorted. The lockbox wants the
digits from badge **V-2071**."*

### 14. Reset Instructions
Return key + Note #1 to the lockbox, relock, and **scramble the dial off 2071**. Re-place the visitor
log and poster. (~30 sec.)

### 15. Estimated Solve Time
**~6 minutes.**
