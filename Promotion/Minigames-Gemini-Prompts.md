# Mini-games: Gemini image prompts

Generate each image in landscape 16:9. Save it as `scene.jpg` in the game's folder. The physical games stay "Coming soon" until their photo is in place; then the click areas are mapped onto the photo.

Shared style for all photos: photorealistic, shot on a 24 mm lens at eye level, natural daylight or realistic indoor light, sharp focus throughout (deep depth of field), no text overlays, no watermarks, no people looking at the camera. Every listed object must be clearly visible, separate from the others and not overlapping, so each one can be clicked on its own.
