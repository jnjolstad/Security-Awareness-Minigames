Visitor Policy

Every visitor MUST:

  1. Sign in and out at this desk.
  2. Have a named NIMBUS host recorded in the visitor log.
  3. Be escorted by their host at all times.

🚩 A visitor who breaks all three rules is an immediate security risk.

Report their BADGE NUMBER to security.

Tailgating / piggybacking — following someone through a door without badging — is how most physical breaches begin. If you’re not sure who someone is, challenge politely and report.
