# Intranet post: BREACH PROTOCOL (digital and physical)

**Title:** BREACH PROTOCOL: Can your team stop BLACKFROST?

**Teaser:** A 45-minute cybersecurity escape room for Security Awareness Month. Play it in person at the Stavanger office or online from wherever you are.

---

A security lead has gone dark. Ransomware called **BLACKFROST** is about to deploy. Somewhere inside the company, an attacker is counting on you to trust the wrong person.

This Security Awareness Month, gather your colleagues for **BREACH PROTOCOL**: an escape room built around investigation, communication and teamwork. Follow the evidence, expose the insider and fire the kill switch before the clock hits zero. Expect suspicious emails, questionable access, rogue Wi-Fi and some convincing red herrings. The obvious answer may not be the right one.

### Two ways to play

**🏢 Physical: Stavanger office**
- A real room full of props, clues and locks
- A host welcomes your team and runs the session
- Only available at the Stavanger office

**💻 Digital: play from anywhere**
- The same mission, fully in the browser
- Gather on one shared screen or a Teams call
- Self-service: your team code starts the clock, no host needed
- Built-in hints if you get stuck (they cost points)

### What you need to know

- **45 minutes**, teams of **3–6 players**
- No cybersecurity or technical experience required
- Sessions on weekdays in **October and November** (12:00–15:00). See the registration calendar
- Registration for each date closes at **16:00 the day before**
- Every team gets **one attempt**, and results go on a leaderboard. Escape fast and use few hints to top it

### How to register

One team captain registers the group: choose a time, add an optional alternative, and tick **“Play the digital version”** if your team will play online. You'll get a confirmation email with your session. Digital teams also get the link and their personal team code.
