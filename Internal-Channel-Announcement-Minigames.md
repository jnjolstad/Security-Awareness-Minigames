# Internal channel post – Mini-games

## Full version

🕵️ **THINK LIKE AN ATTACKER: 10 security mini-games, 10 minutes each**

Attackers don't break in through the strongest door. They look for the propped fire exit, the password on a sticky note, the invoice that "just needs paying today".

For Security Awareness Month you can now switch sides. Each mini-game is a realistic scene with hidden openings an attacker would use. Find them, then pick the move that breaks the attack chain.

**Physical:** 🏢 Weakest link · 🚪 Front door · 🌙 Night shift · ✈️ On the road · 🏠 Home office
**Digital:** ✉️ Inbox · 👤 Overshare · 🖥️ Desktop · 🔗 Share settings · 🎣 Real or fake

**How it works**
- 10 minutes per game, no login, no installs. Works best on a laptop.
- Start with 600 points. The clock ticks, wrong clicks and hints cost, and every opening you miss costs 100.
- Pick a nickname to join the leaderboard, or play without it.
- Your best score on each game counts towards the **Championship**.
- Layouts and answers are shuffled every run, so you can't memorise your way to the top. The leaderboard also shows your **1st try** score.

The openings are tagged with NSM's basic principles for ICT security (*Grunnprinsipper for IKT-sikkerhet*) where they fit, so you learn the real habit behind every click.

➡️ **Play now:**
https://njolstad.no/security-awareness-month/minigames/

Can you top the board? Have a go over lunch, then challenge your team.

**Spot it. Report it. Fix it.**

*Thinking like an attacker is for spotting risk. Testing real systems or premises always needs written permission.*
