# Reset Checklist (between groups)

> Target: **≤ 10 minutes**. Do the **digital reset first** (it runs while you walk the room re-arming
> props). Tick top-to-bottom; nothing here needs tools.

---

## A. Digital reset (≈ 1 min, mostly hands-off)

- [ ] Run **`Phase-3-Computer-Setup/reset.bat`** — it closes the browser (clears all progress), restores
      the evidence files from `_MASTER`, and relaunches the terminal at the **login** screen.
      *(Quick alternative with no file changes: press **Ctrl+Alt+R**, or just close & relaunch the browser.)*
- [ ] Confirm the terminal shows **login**, the case-notes/findings panel is empty, and the countdown is
      **back at 45:00 and NOT counting** (it starts when the host presses **Start Mission**).
- [ ] If you used the standalone tablet `timer.html`, tap its **Reset** control (reloading preserves its state).

---

## B. Physical reset by zone (≈ 6–8 min)

### Zone A — Reception
- [ ] Retrieve **desk key** + **Alex's Note #1** from wherever the team left them; put both **back inside
      the lockbox**.
- [ ] **Relock the lockbox** and **scramble the dial off 2071**.
- [ ] Re-place the **Visitor Sign-In Log** on the clipboard and straighten the **Visitor Policy poster**.

### Zone B — Alex's Workstation
- [ ] Re-stick the **yellow decoy note** (`Summer2023!`) on the monitor bezel.
- [ ] Return the **4 dot-objects** (rocket/cactus/car/lightning bolt) to their spots, dots visible.
- [ ] Put the **A. MERCER nameplate** back on the desk.
- [ ] Return the **Authenticator token** to the drawer; return **Alex's Note #2** to the drawer.
- [ ] If the **MFA recovery envelope** was opened, swap in a **fresh sealed spare**.
- [ ] Close and **relock the desk drawer**; confirm the next team must retrieve its key from the lockbox.

### Zone C — SOC Wall
- [ ] Straighten the **Network Map** + **Wi-Fi Survey** posters (static — nothing consumed).
- [ ] Re-pin the optional **parking-lot photo** if used.

### Zone D — Evidence Shelf
- [ ] Re-tape the **PROJECT FROST USB** under the shelf.
- [ ] Return **Badge Access Log** + **Contractor/HR** pages to the evidence binder.
- [ ] Return the **Cloud-Exfil alert** to the host station; give it out only after a correct accusation.
      Reset the green/amber/red folders and their decoy pages.
- [ ] Re-place the **Riley red-herring** notice + angry sticky on the shelf/corkboard.
- [ ] Reset the **suspect ID badges** on the board.

### Whole room
- [ ] Quick litter/seat sweep; collect stray pens; tidy chairs.
- [ ] Re-home anything a team moved out of zone.

---

## C. Consumables to keep stocked

| Item | Why | Keep on hand |
|---|---|---|
| Sealed **MFA recovery envelopes** | Single-use seal | 3–5 spares |
| Spare labelled **PROJECT FROST USB** | In case one walks off | 1–2 |
| Tape (for the USB) / Blu-Tack (posters) | Re-mounting | always |
| Printed **Quick-Reference card** | Your live cheat sheet | 1 per host |

---

## D. 30-second sanity check before "Go"

- [ ] Lockbox **locked**, dial **off 2071**, key **inside**.
- [ ] Terminal at **login**, countdown **45:00 idle**.
- [ ] USB **taped under shelf**; exfil alert held at the **host station**.
- [ ] Token + sealed envelope + Note #2 **in the drawer**; Note #1 **in the lockbox**.
- [ ] Decoy sticky **on the monitor**; dot-objects **placed**.

All ticked → re-read the intro (file 01) and start the next group.

---

> Pro tip for back-to-back bookings: a **second host (floater)** can run this checklist while the first
> host debriefs the previous team — keeps your turnaround near 10 minutes.
