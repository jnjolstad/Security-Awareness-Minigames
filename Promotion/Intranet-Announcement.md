## Full post

**REGISTRATION IS OPEN: BREACH PROTOCOL**

A security analyst has disappeared. Confidential data is at risk. Somewhere inside the company, an attacker is counting on you to trust the wrong person.

This Security Awareness Month, you and your colleagues are invited to take part in **BREACH PROTOCOL**: a 45-minute cybersecurity escape room built around investigation, communication and teamwork.

Your mission is to follow the evidence, expose the insider and stop BLACKFROST before the clock reaches zero. Expect suspicious emails, questionable access, physical evidence and more than a few convincing red herrings. The obvious answer may not be the right one.

### What you need to know

- **45-minute** escape-room mission
- Teams of **3-6 players**
- No cybersecurity or technical experience required
- Available sessions are shown in the registration calendar
- Registration for each date closes at **16:00 the day before**

### How to register

Choose one team captain to register the group. Select your preferred date and time in the calendar, add an optional alternative, and submit the team details. Session availability will be confirmed by email.

**Register your team:**  
https://www.njolstad.no/security-awareness-month-escape-room/

**Trust nothing. Verify everything. Beat the clock.**
